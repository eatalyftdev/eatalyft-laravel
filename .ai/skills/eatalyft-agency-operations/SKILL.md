# EataLyft Agency Operations Skill

## Overview
Multi-branch agency management for transport operators.

## Key Features
- Agency dashboard with KPI cards
- Branch/station management
- Staff invite system (sub_agent/teller)
- Fleet management (75/65/50/30/18 seaters)
- Trip scheduling across operating cities
- Commission ledger and payouts

## Staff Assignments
- staff_assignments table for multi-branch support
- Primary assignment synced to users.agency_id/branch_id
- Active assignment-based authorization
